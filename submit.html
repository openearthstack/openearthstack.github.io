Ground truth collection

Every location tells us something satellites cannot.

We are starting with a simple idea — that data collection does not need to be expensive or slow. Most people across Africa already visit the places that need to be on the map every day. We want to understand what it looks like when they contribute what they see.

First stage · Experimenting

This is the beginning of something larger. We are testing whether a simple, low-friction contribution model can become the foundation of an on-demand ground truth network for African markets. Your submission helps us understand what that could look like.
