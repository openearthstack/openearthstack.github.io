Sentinel-1 Acquisition Modes.

Sentinel-1 collects in four modes, each optimised for a different class of target. The mode in your scene determines what you can do with it.

IW — Interferometric Wide

The default operational mode over land. 250 km swath, roughly 5 × 20 m resolution, VV+VH or HH+HV dual-polarisation. Uses TOPSAR (Terrain Observation with Progressive Scans) across three sub-swaths. Most Sentinel-1 archive products over land are IW. Suitable for InSAR time series with 12-day revisit, or 6-day while both S1A and S1B were operational.

EW — Extra Wide

400 km swath, roughly 20 × 40 m resolution. Dual-polarisation HH+HV or VV+VH. Primary mode over polar regions, sea ice, and maritime areas where coverage outweighs resolution. Used operationally for iceberg tracking, marine oil spill detection, and large-area flood mapping.

SM — Stripmap

80 km swath, roughly 5 × 5 m resolution. Single- or dual-polarisation. On-demand, rarely tasked; used for targeted high-resolution coverage such as emergency response. Not available in the routine archive.

WV — Wave

20 × 20 km vignettes along the orbit track, roughly 5 × 5 m resolution, single polarisation (VV or HH alternating). Dedicated to open-ocean wave spectra retrieval. The OCN product is derived from WV mode.

For most terrestrial SAR workloads (land cover, flood mapping, InSAR deformation), you will work with IW GRD or IW SLC scenes. For maritime and polar monitoring, EW is standard.
