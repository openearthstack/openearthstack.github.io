How is OpenEarthStack different from other SAR data providers?

Commercial SAR has typically been priced and packaged for a narrow set of asset classes and geographies. We deliver analysis-ready SAR and derived data products across the contexts operators work in every day: tailings dams, pipelines, urban subsidence, and linear transport networks.

Can the data pipeline run on-site or offline?

Yes. Our processing pipeline runs on 8 GB of RAM, producing analysis-ready output in under 20 seconds per scene. The same workflow deploys to cloud, on-prem, or on-site hardware.

What sensors and asset classes do you cover?

Our data products are derived from Sentinel-1 C-band InSAR and amplitude by default; commercial SAR integrations are available on request. Coverage spans dams, tailings facilities, pipelines, bridges, highways, and dense urban subsidence regimes across 18 countries and three continents.

Is the platform interoperable?

Interoperable by architecture. Built on standard geospatial interfaces like STAC, COG, Zarr, and OGC APIs, so it integrates directly into existing geospatial pipelines. Data ships STAC-catalogued, ready to drop into your analytics tooling.

How do you validate coverage on a specific asset or region?

Data quality is characterised per delivery: coherence, geometry, temporal baseline, and scene-level QA, decomposed by asset class and region. For a specific portfolio we scope expected SAR sensitivity against your asset inventory before commitment.

What does a commercial engagement look like?

A short scoping conversation, a pilot delivery against your operating context, then production on the delivery mode that fits: cloud, on-prem, or on-site. Typical engagements go from scoping to production in weeks.
