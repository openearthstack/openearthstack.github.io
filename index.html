How it works

Our sophisticated platform blends machine learning with human intelligence.

Contributors already on the ground in African markets collect verified location data through our mobile platform — capturing what no satellite or survey can.

Our data science layer then structures, validates, and enriches that data into actionable market intelligence — delivered fast, at scale, across any African geography.
