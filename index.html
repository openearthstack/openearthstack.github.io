How is OpenEarthStack different from other SAR analytics providers?

Most commercial SAR stacks are optimised for a narrow set of asset classes and geographies. We build and benchmark ours across the contexts operators run every day: tailings dams, pipelines, urban subsidence, and linear transport networks.

Can the models run offline at the edge?

Yes. The full inference pipeline runs offline on 8 GB of RAM, producing analysis-ready output in under 20 seconds per scene. The same workflow deploys to cloud, on-prem, or a site office.

What sensors and asset classes do you cover?

Models train on Sentinel-1 C-band InSAR and amplitude products by default, with extensions for commercial SAR on request. Benchmarks cover dams, tailings facilities, pipelines, bridges, highways, and dense urban subsidence regimes across 18 countries and three continents.

Is the platform open and interoperable?

Open by architecture. The stack sits on standard geospatial interfaces (STAC, COG, Zarr, OGC APIs), so it drops into the tooling your team already runs. Datasets ship STAC-catalogued, and every released model carries its benchmarks with it.

How do you validate performance on a specific asset or region?

Every released model ships with benchmarks decomposed by asset class, biome, land cover, and acquisition geometry. For a specific portfolio we run targeted validation against jointly sourced ground truth, with pass criteria agreed upfront.

What does a commercial engagement look like?

A scoping conversation, a pilot against your operating context, then production on the delivery mode that fits: cloud, on-prem, or edge. Timelines run in weeks.
