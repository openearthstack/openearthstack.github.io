What We Build

Data infrastructure and intelligence tools, built together.

01

Data Infrastructure

Raw SAR data is not usable out of the box. Getting from acquisition to analysis takes six or more processing steps. We handle all of it, so what arrives in your pipeline is clean, analysis-ready data you can work with immediately.

02

Intelligence Layers

Deformation detection, flood mapping, structural change, damage assessment. We build the AI models and processing pipelines that turn analysis-ready data into answers people can act on, trained and evaluated on the geographies where they will actually be used.

The Problem

Satellites already cover the earth every few days. Unlike optical, radar sees through cloud cover, at night, and in the conditions where ground-truth matters most. What is missing is the infrastructure to make it usable, and AI models trained on the geographies where they are actually needed. No one is building both layers, starting where the need is greatest. We are.

Frequently asked.

How is OpenEarthStack different from an existing SAR operator or EO platform?

No one else is building both the data infrastructure and the intelligence tools together, starting from the contexts where the need is greatest. Most existing tooling assumes Western infrastructure, Western datasets, and Western expertise. We start from the other end.

Will this run on my infrastructure?

Yes. Our processing pipeline runs on 8 GB of RAM, producing analysis-ready output in under 20 seconds per scene. The same workflow deploys to cloud, on-prem, or on-site hardware. That is a real differentiator in contexts where bandwidth, power, and compute cannot be assumed.

What sensors and asset classes do you support?

We work with freely available global SAR data that already covers the entire planet every few days, and extend to commercial sources when a specific target demands it. Coverage spans disaster response, agriculture, infrastructure, environmental monitoring, and maritime. Sensor-agnostic by design: we match the right source to the task.

Will it plug into the tools my team already uses?

Interoperable by architecture. Built on standard geospatial interfaces like STAC, COG, Zarr, and OGC APIs, so it integrates directly into existing geospatial pipelines. Data ships STAC-catalogued, ready to drop into your analytics tooling.

How do you make sure the models work for my region?

We train and evaluate on the geographies where the models will actually be used, and characterise quality per delivery.

How fast will I see results?

A short scoping conversation, a pilot delivery against your operating context, then production on the delivery mode that fits: cloud, on-prem, or on-site. Typical engagements go from scoping to production in weeks.
