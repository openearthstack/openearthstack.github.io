How is OpenEarthStack different from other SAR analytics providers?

Most SAR stacks optimise for a narrow set of well-funded deployment contexts. We build foundation models benchmarked explicitly across underrepresented geographies, with documented per-context performance so teams know how a model will behave where they actually operate.

Can the models run offline at the edge?

Yes. Our inference pipelines run fully offline on consumer hardware with 8 GB of RAM, producing analysis-ready output in under 20 seconds per scene. Built for field deployment, not just data centres.

What sensors and geographies do you cover?

Published datasets span 18 countries across three continents, prioritising regions with the thinnest labelled SAR coverage. Models are trained primarily on Sentinel-1 C-band, with extensions for commercial SAR sources on request.

Is the platform open and interoperable?

Open by architecture, not by licence. The stack is built on standard geospatial interfaces, so it integrates with the tooling your team already runs, no platform migration, no proprietary format lock-in. Datasets are STAC-catalogued and benchmarks are published alongside every released model.

How do you validate model performance for a new region?

Every released model ships with benchmarks broken down by biome, land cover, and acquisition geometry. For novel contexts, we run validation against ground truth provided or sourced jointly before any deployment decision.

What does an engagement typically look like?

A short scoping conversation, followed by a pilot against your operating context, then production deployment on whatever hardware profile fits your environment. Timelines are weeks, not quarters.
